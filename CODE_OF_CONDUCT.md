# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in PromptLens a harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback and focus disagreements on ideas and evidence.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing private information and sustained disruption. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. A dedicated contact address will be published before accepting external contributions.

This policy is based on Contributor Covenant 2.1. The canonical text is available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
